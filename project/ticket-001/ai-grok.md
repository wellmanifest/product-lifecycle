# Participant: grok (AI agent)

## Understanding

SaaS lifecycle owns commercial state (trial, subscription, provisioning) but
not the catalog of what is being sold. Product identity, stage and
jurisdiction-aware availability need their own versioned DSL so a later SaaS
offer can bind `productRef` values without absorbing legal or billing
authority.

## Execution plan

1. Adopt published `wellmanifest/new-project` and create one local seed
   baseline before any standard file exists.
2. Define closed catalog, request, product-state and receipt variants.
3. Constrain requests with matching GBNF and reject prices, marketing copy,
   credentials and deployment coordinates.
4. Document composition with `legal-lifecycle` and `saas-lifecycle`.
5. Validate locally, through the governance gate and in networkless Docker.

## Risks and controls

- A generally-available product can be offered where law forbids it;
  availability is explicit and fail-closed against the bound legal pack.
- A catalog can smuggle prices or settlement; commercial amounts stay in
  saas-lifecycle.
- Sunset can be silent; withdrawn and sunset are distinct honest states.
